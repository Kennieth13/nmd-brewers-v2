ABOUT US

The Story Behind NMD Brewers

NMD Brewers was founded by Kennieth, inspired by his love for coffee and the happiness he feels whenever he visits a coffee shop to reunite with his friends.

That simple experience inspired him to create a coffee shop where people can enjoy good coffee, relax, and spend time with the people they care about.

NMD Brewers is more than just a coffee shop. It is a place for good coffee, good company, and happy memories.

Good Coffee, Made for Everyone

At NMD Brewers, we focus on serving coffee and drinks that people can enjoy every day. We offer different flavors and choices to match different preferences.

We believe that good coffee can be part of simple moments, whether you're taking a break, meeting friends, or starting your day.

A Place to Relax and Connect

NMD Brewers is more than a place to get coffee. It is a comfortable space where people can take a break, meet with friends, or simply enjoy their drink.
